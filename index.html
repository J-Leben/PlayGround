HOW TO PLAY

Drag from the basketball toward where you want to send it. Drag farther for more power, then release. Read the dotted flight guide, find the arc, and sink at least 3 out of 5 to level up.

Desktop — drag with your mouse, or use ← → and Space.
Mobile — drag your finger from the ball toward the hoop.
Progression — earn XP and coins for makes. Advance through rounds to unlock new basketballs.
